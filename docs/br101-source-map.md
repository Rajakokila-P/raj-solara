## Editorial boundaries

The original deck describes perpetual operation, infinite endurance, zero latency and uninterrupted connections. Those have not been demonstrated. They are identified as ambitions instead of advertised as product capabilities. Satellite behaviour is simulated locally without a network. No certified aircraft, safe flight controller, engineering design or deployable autonomous vehicle is supplied.

The new BR101 capacities, areas and demand are teaching assumptions created for the website, not measurements from the deck. Three earlier catalogue concepts remain available and retain compatible storage identifiers.
